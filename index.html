    Recommended ESP32 Touchscreen Displays for Your Project

    This free ESP32 touchscreen selector helps you answer a practical question: which ESP32 touchscreen board is most suitable for the project you are actually building?

    Start with a use case such as a Home Assistant dashboard, HMI / control panel, sensor dashboard, battery-powered device, wearable, LVGL GUI, camera / AI vision or audio interface. Then refine the recommendation with technical requirements such as MCU, display size, resolution, interface, touch, PSRAM and LVGL support.

    ESP32-S3 vs ESP32-P4

    Choose ESP32-S3 for many conventional touchscreen projects where simplicity, broad board availability and proven graphics support matter. Choose ESP32-P4 when your project genuinely benefits from advanced graphics, MIPI-DSI, camera/MIPI-CSI, larger HMI displays or multimedia-oriented hardware.

    The catalog is deliberately conservative: unknown specifications remain unknown rather than being guessed. Verify exact controller, pinout, voltage, memory, interface and driver support before purchase.
